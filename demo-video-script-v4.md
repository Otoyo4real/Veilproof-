# VeilProof demo video script v4 — Torch style

Model: the Torch video (Bima Jadiva, NEAR Hackathon, 1:47, 16:9, 720p).
Dark background, motion graphics + dark-mode screen recording, large bold
white centred text one phrase per beat, small bottom-centre step captions,
fast pacing (one idea every 5-10s, hard cuts), continuous brisk English
voiceover, no music standing out. Structure: problem -> solution ->
one simple model -> demo in imperative steps -> trust -> CTA.
Each point is said once in voiceover, once in large on-screen text, and
once by showing it in the UI. Plain short declarative second-person
English. No jargon left unexplained. The demo, not the theory, is the
longest section.

Format: 16:9 landscape, 1920x1080, ~100 seconds. Accent: VeilProof teal.
End card: VeilProof logo, white pill button "Prove your wallet",
veilproof22.vercel.app below, "Live on NEAR Testnet" with a green dot.

## 0:00-0:02 Hook

On screen (huge white, centred): "You need to prove it."
Voiceover: "You need a loan. The lender asks for your wallet."

## 0:02-0:15 Problem, shown concretely

On screen: a wallet address, then a block explorer showing every
balance, every transfer, every counterparty. Floor-style red numbers.
Voiceover: "Your wallet address is your whole financial life. Every
balance. Every transfer. Every counterparty. Hand it over, and anyone
can watch your money, forever."
On screen text: "Your wallet is your whole financial life."

## 0:15-0:30 Pivot, solution

On screen: "What if you could prove it, without handing it over?"
Voiceover: "So what if you could prove you're good for it, without
handing over your wallet? Introducing VeilProof."
On screen text: "Introducing VeilProof."

## 0:30-0:45 Core idea in one simple model

On screen: diagram, wallet on the left, a score in the middle, a public
report on the right with no address in it. Score 770, band Solid.
Voiceover: "VeilProof reads your wallet onchain and scores it. The
lender sees the score, not your money. Your address never goes public.
The amounts stay sealed."
On screen text: "A score, not your wallet."

## 0:45-1:20 Demo walkthrough, imperative steps in the real product

Each step 10-15 seconds, shown in the live prove page, not described
in the abstract. Small bottom-centre caption labels each step.

1. "Connect your wallet. One click."
   Caption: "Connect your wallet"
   Screen: step 1, Connect wallet, account connected, "Proving:
   clearsite587.testnet".
2. "Pick what to prove. Balance, volume, history."
   Caption: "Pick what to prove"
   Screen: step 2, policy form filled in.
3. "Get your score. 770. Solid."
   Caption: "Get your score"
   Screen: evaluation results, claims passing, score 770, band Solid.
4. "Build the report. The address stays hidden."
   Caption: "Build the report"
   Screen: step 3, report built, no address in the public report.
5. "Publish the hashes onchain. Anyone can verify it."
   Caption: "Publish onchain"
   Screen: step 4, publish transaction, report hash anchored.

## 1:20-1:40 Trust close and call to action

On screen: "Verified onchain. Private by design."
Voiceover: "Every claim is verified against the chain. The AI analysis
is attested. The report hash is anchored on NEAR. And your address
never appears. Privacy is the point. VeilProof. Prove you're good for
it, without handing over your wallet."
End card: VeilProof logo, white pill button "Prove your wallet",
veilproof22.vercel.app, "Live on NEAR Testnet" with a green dot.
