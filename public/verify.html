Verify a report

Someone sent you a VeilProof report — a lender, a grant committee, a deal partner. Check it here in one click. No account, no wallet, nothing leaves your browser except two read-only view calls to the NEAR testnet RPC.

The demo loads a real published claim from the demo wallet and verifies it against its onchain record — all seven checks should pass, including the AI summary. The report proves claims about a wallet's onchain history — for example "balance at least 5 NEAR" — without revealing which wallet to the public.

What verification proves. The report hash, evidence hash and policy hash match the record published on NEAR, so the report was not altered after publication. The data hash is recomputed from the disclosure snapshot, and the claims are re-run from that snapshot, so the claims follow from chain data the verifier can re-fetch. The wallet address is never shown here — only its hash commitment. What it does not prove. Nothing about data that never touched the chain. A report with no AI summary means "not analysed", not "verified by AI".
