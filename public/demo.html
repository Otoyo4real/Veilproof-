Try breaking it

Verification is only worth something if tampering fails. On the verifier portal, paste the demo report, change one number, and verify again: the report hash and the claims check fail, and the page says so plainly.

Ten checks, not one

The verifier reruns the binding, the hashes, the claims, the score and the validity, and reports each one separately.

Five statuses

Source data, calculations, attestation, report integrity and privacy each get their own status, so a report can pass four and fail one.

Privacy, measured

The public report carries thresholds and a score band only. The privacy panel says what it discloses and what it keeps private.
