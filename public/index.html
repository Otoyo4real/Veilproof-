Prove you’re good for it — without handing over your wallet

A lender, a deal partner, a grant committee — they all want proof before they say yes. Today that proof is your wallet address, and your address is your whole financial life: every balance, every transfer, everyone you have ever paid. VeilProof changes the deal. You prove the one fact they need — you hold at least 1 NEAR, your record is clean — and the world never learns which wallet is yours. The facts come from the chain itself, so they cannot be faked. Only a hash of your address is ever published; the address itself goes only to the verifier you chose, in a private package.

Your wallet stays yours. Everything on this page runs in your browser. The optional AI analysis sees claim-level facts only — never your address, never transaction hashes. A report without an AI summary says "not analysed", never "verified by AI".
1

Connect your wallet (NEAR testnet)

Not connected. Meteor and MyNearWallet are supported.

2

Prove you own the wallet (optional, recommended)

Sign a short ownership challenge with the connected wallet. The signature is shown here as evidence; the publish transaction in step 6 is the binding proof onchain (the registry records the signer as the claim owner).

3

Fetch your onchain snapshot

Paste the transaction hashes you want the claims evaluated over, one per line. A bare hash is assumed to be yours; use hash,senderId for incoming transfers sent by someone else. Hashes that cannot be verified onchain are skipped.

4

Choose the policy, evaluate the claims

Leave a field blank to skip that claim. Thresholds are in NEAR. Windows are in days. A minimum tx count or volume needs its window.

5

Disclose, analyse, build the report

Choose which evaluated claims go into the public report. Claims you leave out are simply absent from the report — the verifier never sees them unless you disclose them another way.

AI analysis not run yet. The report will say "not analysed" unless you run it.

6

Publish the hashes onchain

Publishing is a real testnet transaction, signed by your wallet. Only the three hashes and the report id go onchain — never your address, never amounts, never transactions. The contract charges storage at cost and refunds any excess deposit.
